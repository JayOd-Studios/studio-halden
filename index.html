02 — The studio

Small on purpose.

We take six projects a year, and each one is drawn by the people who will visit the site. That is the whole model.

Studio Halden was founded in 2014 after ten years of working for larger offices. The work has stayed deliberately close to home: houses for people we know, a church restoration, a library that had to fit between two existing walls.

03 — Process

Four stages, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Walk the site

    Before drawings, a morning on site: light, wind, noise, and the route people already take through it.

  2. 02

    One model, at 1:200

    A physical model before a render. It is faster to be wrong in cardboard than in a picture.

  3. 03

    Draw it twice

    Once for the planning authority, once for the builder. If the two disagree, the design is not finished.

  4. 04

    Stay until it stands

    Weekly site visits through construction. The last detail is usually decided on a scaffold, not in the office.
